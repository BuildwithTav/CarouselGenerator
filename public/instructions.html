Step 01

Welcome to Carousel Studio

This is what you see when you first log in. Everything is built around one simple flow — set up your brand once, then generate carousels whenever you need them.
Step 02

Set up your Brand tab first

Click Brand in the top navigation. Do this before you generate anything — it's what makes every carousel look and sound like you.
  • 1Upload your profile photo — this appears on every slide
  • 2Enter your display name, handle and website URL
  • 3Toggle on blue tick and website if you want them shown on slides
  • 4Select your industry so the AI understands your niche
  • 5Fill in your Voice Profile — describe your tone, what to avoid, and how you like to write. The more specific you are, the better the output
  • 6On the right, upload up to 10 cover photos or search thousands of free stock images to use on your cover slide
  • 7Adjust photo opacity and overlay, choose white or dark text, then select your badge and headline position
Step 03

Customise your slide visuals

Click Visual in the navigation. This controls how slides 2 onwards look — backgrounds, fonts, colours and more.
  • 1Choose your headline style — Bold, Uppercase, Serif or Minimal
  • 2Pick your brand accent colour from the presets or add a custom hex code
  • 3Select your body font
  • 4Set your slide background — Dark, Light, a custom colour from the picker, or upload your own image
  • 5Use the White text or Dark text toggle to make sure text is readable on your chosen background
  • 6The live preview updates as you make changes so you always know what you're getting
Step 04

Generate your carousel

Click Generate in the navigation. Fill in the details and hit the button — your carousel is ready in 15–25 seconds.
  • 1Select who this carousel is aimed at — Your Customers/Clients or Industry Peers
  • 2Enter your topic. Hit the dice icon for AI to suggest one, or upload a screenshot of a carousel you like as inspiration
  • 3Add a brief with any specific instructions — exact title, slide structure, tone, or anything else you want included
  • 4Select your text density — Punchy (short and sharp), Balanced, or Depth (more detail)
  • 5Choose your format and number of slides, then click Generate Carousel
Step 05

Edit your slides

Once generated, all your slides appear on screen. Everything is editable before you download.
  • 1Click any slide to select it and edit the content in the right panel
  • 2Edit the slide title, headline, accent word, body text and CTA
  • 3Use AI Rewrite to improve any slide — type an instruction like "make this punchier" or "add a specific stat"
  • 4Switch between 4:5 and 9:16 format at the top
  • 5Use Regenerate in the top right to regenerate the whole carousel if needed
Step 06

Generate caption and download

When you're happy with your slides, generate your caption and download everything ready to post.
  • 1Scroll down and click Generate Caption — it creates a ready-to-post caption with hashtags based on your carousel content
  • 2Click Copy to copy the caption directly to your clipboard
  • 3Click Download Slide 1 to download just the cover, or Download All (zip) to get all slides as PNG files in one folder
  • 4Upload your slides and paste your caption straight into Instagram, LinkedIn or TikTok
Bonus

Quote Cards — a bonus feature

Click Quotes in the navigation. Create up to 3 branded quote cards in seconds.
  • 1Choose your card template — Classic, Notebook, Feminine or Raw
  • 2Set your background, signature, font and text colour
  • 3Enter a label word that appears above the quote — you can also upload your own custom images or templates to use as quote card backgrounds
  • 4The live preview updates on the right as you make changes
Bonus

Generate and download your quote cards

Scroll down to enter your quotes or let AI generate them for you.
  • 1Choose On Brand (quotes based on your industry and brand voice) or Life & Mindset
  • 2Hit Generate and 3 quotes appear instantly — or type your own into the slots
  • 3Preview all 3 cards live before downloading
  • 4Download individual cards or all 3 at once as PNG files
Account

Your account, credits and affiliate link

Click Account in the navigation to see your plan, track your credits and find your affiliate link.
  • 1Your current plan and credits remaining are shown at the top — credits reset every month
  • 2Copy your personal affiliate link and share it anywhere — social media, email, DMs, communities
  • 3Every time someone signs up through your link and upgrades to a paid plan, you earn recurring commission
  • 4Track your total earned, pending commissions and paid out amounts from your dashboard
