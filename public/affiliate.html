How far do commissions go?

Tier 1 — You earn on everyone you refer directly. No cap on how many you can refer.

Tier 2 — You earn on everyone your referrals refer. No cap on how many Tier 2 referrals you can have.

Commissions stop at Tier 2. If your Tier 2 referrals go on to refer people themselves, you do not earn on that layer — but they do. Everyone wins at the level they operate.

The honest answer: If you actively use Carousel Studio for your own content AND refer aggressively — Agency at $100/mo earns you slightly more (40% vs 35%) and gives you 3,000 credits. If you want zero outgoings and 100% passive income — the Affiliate Licence wins. One $297 payment and you never pay another penny. Both are compelling. Only you know which fits your situation.
